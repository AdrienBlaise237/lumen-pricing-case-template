03 / The recommendation

Balanced scenario · launch at €2.19

Premium enough to signal quality.
Accessible enough to earn trial.

Start in Gym & Office and DTC Online, then use repeat and CAC evidence before expanding to grocery. Lead with clean energy for active urban routines—not a luxury adaptogen ritual.

Deliberate trade-off: we give up some early grocery scale and the €2.59 margin ceiling in exchange for materially stronger acceptance, faster learning, and more credible premium positioning.
